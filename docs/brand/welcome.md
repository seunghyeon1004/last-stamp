**Title:** Welcome to Last Stamp: how to play (read me first)

---

Every day at 00:00 UTC a new card goes up here. It's a 5x5 picture, plus the rubber stamps that can make it.

**Your job:** copy the card in as few presses as you can.

**How to play**

1. Open today's post and tap **Start stamping**.
2. Pick a stamp from the tray, then tap your card where it should land. A dashed outline shows the spot.
3. Tap the same spot again, or hit **Press**.
4. **Lift** undoes one press, and **New card** starts over. Hold **Target** to compare your card with the goal.

**The one rule:** stamps are opaque, so **the last stamp wins**. Wherever two stamps overlap, the later one is what you see. Most cards need you to work out what went down first.

**Par** is the true minimum number of presses for the day. A solver checks every card, so on par means you found the best possible order.

Cards start gentle on Monday (2 stamps) and get harder through the week (4 stamps by Saturday).

**After you finish (both optional):**
- **Comment my result** posts your press count and colour strip as a reply under the pinned results comment.
- **Wear as flair** shows your earned title here: Apprentice, then Inker, Stampsmith and Master Stamper.

Streaks count when you finish the day's own card. Spoilers about the order are welcome in the results thread. Please use spoiler tags in the first hours.

Have fun, and see you at tomorrow's card.
